# Domain Expert Face-Validation Protocol

Status: **TEMPLATE — execution and signatures pending**.

## Participants and independence

Record each participant's role, warehouse experience, organization, conflicts,
date, and whether they contributed to model development. At least one reviewer
must understand the actual order-release, batching, routing, staffing, and SLA
process represented by the model.

## Materials supplied before the session

1. Research questions and intended use.
2. Model boundary, flow diagram, assumptions, and stopping policy.
3. Parameter provenance and operational data report.
4. Algorithm pseudocode without favorable-result rankings.
5. Hand traces and extreme-condition evidence.
6. Proposed baseline, factor levels, MPID, and validation tolerances.

## Structured review

For every entity, queue, event, resource, routing constraint, time component,
deadline rule, staffing behavior, congestion omission, and KPI denominator, the
expert records `accurate`, `acceptable simplification`, or `material defect`,
with rationale. Reviewers then walk through low demand, normal demand, overload,
urgent orders, exact cart capacity, shift-end backlog, picker absence, and layout
edge cases.

Validation tolerances and operationally important differences must be agreed
before revealing method rankings. Every material defect receives an owner,
resolution, retest evidence, and closure decision.

## Decision record

- Model scope accepted / conditionally accepted / rejected: pending
- Approved validity domain: pending
- Approved baseline and comparator set: pending
- Approved factor ranges: pending
- Approved MPID and KPI tolerances: pending
- Open limitations: pending
- Expert name/signature/date: pending
- PI name/signature/date: pending

Unsigned minutes or informal verbal feedback do not satisfy Gate A or Gate C.
