# Table 2. Mapping of Literature to Research Focus and Identified Gaps

| Research focus | Key studies | Timing decisions addressed? | Gap addressed by this study |
|---|---|---|---|
| Batch composition | Azadnia et al. (2013); Henn and Schmid (2013); Bustillo et al. (2015); Chen et al. (2016) | No - timing treated as fixed parameter | This study evaluates state-aware release policies and separately crosses timing with composition; it does not claim to solve a globally optimal timing problem. |
| Batch sequencing | Henn (2015); Menendez et al. (2017); Scholz et al. (2017) | No - focus on sequencing after batches formed | This study incorporates due-time urgency in both release and, for integrated policies, order selection. |
| Integrated composition and routing | Van Gils et al. (2018); Cano (2020) | No - assume batching already triggered | This study constrains release by unreserved idle-picker slots and evaluates routing and cart-capacity treatments; congestion and integrated routing optimisation remain outside scope. |
| Online batching | Schleyer and Gue (2012); Cals et al. (2021); Beeks et al. (2022) | Partially - fixed windows or simple thresholds | This study compares transparent state-aware policies using a reproducible finite-horizon simulation and reports where timing and composition effects differ. |
| Same-day delivery context | Voccia et al. (2019); Kuhn et al. (2021) | No - focus on logistics, not warehouse operations | This study addresses the specific release and composition requirements of SDD within warehouse operations. |
