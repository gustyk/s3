# Baseline and Benchmark Plan

FCFS+FTWB is the candidate simple operational baseline; its final designation
requires PI/domain approval. Existing MaxPicker/MaxCart provide utilization
heuristics, EDT provides a standard due-date rule, and DVNS/MPBH are proposed
advanced methods. Fair comparison requires identical demand streams, routing,
capacity, staffing, horizon, KPI definitions, and implementation quality.

Before submission:

- justify at least one simple and one strong literature/domain benchmark;
- reproduce benchmark definitions exactly or document deviations;
- verify each implementation with published examples where available;
- compare outcome magnitude, uncertainty, and computational cost;
- report every configuration where a proposed method loses or ties;
- do not claim state-of-the-art without a systematic, current literature review.
