institutionally accepted JCR/SJR edition immediately before submission.

## Recommended decision rule

1. Choose **Simulation Modelling Practice and Theory** if the validated paper's
   novelty is primarily a rigorous DES methodology/application and transparent
   V&V workflow.
2. Choose **Computers & Industrial Engineering** or **IJPR** if operational data
   establish a generalizable warehouse decision-aid contribution and the
   advanced heuristics beat strong literature baselines with practical effect.
3. Consider **EJOR** only after an independent OR reviewer confirms that the
   algorithmic contribution is substantial enough for a methodology paper.

## Sources and submission obligations

- SMPT: official aims and scope and guide for authors
  (`https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/simulation-modelling-practice-and-theory`);
  application papers are expected to make model development, implementation,
  scalability, and real-data V&V transparent. The guide requires research-data
  linking/statement and standard declarations.
- CAIE: official aims and scope and guide
  (`https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/computers-and-industrial-engineering`);
  the guide requires repository deposit/link or a reason data cannot be shared,
  a data-availability statement, declarations, and anonymized review files.
- IJPR: official journal scope
  (`https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/tprs20`); explicitly covers production,
  logistics, intralogistics, warehousing, and state-of-the-art comparison.
- EJOR: official aims and author guide
  (`https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/european-journal-of-operational-research`);
  code for optimization, simulation, and data processing should be deposited
  with clear execution instructions.

The corresponding author must still record the exact word/figure limits,
article type, template, APC route, AI-use policy, and required declarations from
the live author guide after selecting one target. No target is marked selected
without PI/coauthor approval.
