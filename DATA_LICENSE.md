# Data licensing and provenance

The quarantined legacy dataset is repository-owned research output. Its source
data provenance and redistribution permission have not yet been documented;
therefore it must not be redistributed outside the research team until the
principal investigator confirms the applicable rights.

New synthetic simulation outputs produced solely by this repository may be
released under CC BY 4.0 after the input-data provenance review is complete.
Real operational data, if later introduced, must remain excluded unless a
separate data-use agreement explicitly permits release.
